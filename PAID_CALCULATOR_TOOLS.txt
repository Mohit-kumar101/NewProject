PRICING MODEL (site-wide)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Free        $0          Basic calc, limited or no saves, SEO traffic
Pro         $9–12/mo    Unlimited saves, PDF export, scenarios A/B/C
Business    $19–29/mo   Branded PDF, 3–5 seats, shared templates

RULE OF THUMB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FREE  = one answer, leave, generic math, converters
PAID  = save history, PDF/report, compare scenarios, libraries, team use

NO PAID APIs REQUIRED for MVP launch.
All tools below run on formulas + user inputs (browser-side).
Optional APIs later: Stripe (billing), Resend (email PDF), tax/fuel data feeds.

SECTION C — WHAT IS FREE VS PAID INSIDE EACH PAID TOOL
================================================================================

FREE TIER (per paid tool)
  - Full calculation once / limited runs
  - See results on screen
  - No or very limited saves (1–3)
  - CalculioHub watermark if PDF allowed at all

PRO ($9–12/mo)
  - Unlimited calculations
  - Unlimited saves / history
  - Scenario compare (A vs B vs C)
  - PDF / CSV export
  - No watermark (or light brand)

BUSINESS ($19–29/mo)
  - Everything in Pro
  - Custom logo / company name on PDF
  - 3–5 team seats
  - Shared templates / price libraries
  - Priority: sales-facing tools (solar, HVAC, remodel ballpark, bid compare)


================================================================================
SECTION D — WHY THESE ARE PAID (SUMMARY)
================================================================================

People pay when the tool:
  1. Is used WEEKLY or PER JOB (bid, load, recipe, payroll week)
  2. Stores THEIR data (recipes, trucks, properties, rate cards)
  3. Produces a SHAREABLE artifact (PDF for client, partner, accountant)
  4. Avoids a costly mistake (losing load, underbid, bad menu price)
  5. Is cheaper/faster than Excel + full SaaS ($49–$199+)

People expect FREE when:
  - One-off curiosity math
  - Generic converters
  - Results they won't reopen tomorrow
